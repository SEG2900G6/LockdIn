LockdIn

What we offer

LockdIn matches you with a tutor who fits the way you learn. Our tutors are university students who did very well in the courses they teach, so they know the material and the exams.

Besides personalized tutoring, you also get study notes, past exams with their correction keys and AI-powered practice questions.

We offer tutoring in:

  • Math
  • Physics
  • Chemistry

Our partners and how we operate

We work with high schools, student associations and libraries to reach the students who need help the most.

Every new student fills out a short questionnaire. LockdIn uses the answers to match them with the right tutor, then they book their sessions online or in person.

After each session, the student rates the tutor so we can keep the quality of our tutoring high.

User statistics and ratings
